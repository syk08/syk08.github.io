Research interests

Three questions I would like to work on in a PhD, drawn from what I have already built and measured.

01

Reliable, scalable data systems

I am interested in how data is collected, synchronised, stored and served when a stable network cannot be assumed. Working on offline-first data collection and a national dashboard platform turned that interest into concrete questions about distributed data systems and data management.

Distributed data systems Data management Offline-first sync Schema migration
02

NLP for low-resource and code-mixed languages

Bangla–English code-mixing is ordinary online and badly served by existing corpora and models. I build annotated datasets for it, and I ask what makes such data reliable enough to train and evaluate on.

Code-mixing Corpus construction Annotation quality Bangla
03

Evaluating language models on pragmatic tasks

Sarcasm, humour and offence turn on what a speaker implies rather than says, and models remain weak at them. I benchmark fine-tuned and zero-shot models on these tasks and on task-oriented dialogue, to find where the models fail and where the evaluation itself does.

LLM evaluation Zero-shot Pragmatics Dialogue

Research experience

  • Undergraduate thesis

    MixSarc: a Bangla–English code-mixed corpus for implicit meaning identification

    Islamic University of Technology · preprint under review at PLOS

    • Built the first public Bangla–English code-mixed corpus annotated for humour, sarcasm, offensiveness and vulgarity: 9,087 sentences drawn from 31,129 scraped social media posts, filtered with script-based rules and an mBERT code-mix classifier.
    • Recruited and coordinated 18 annotators using a qualification test and written guidelines; labels come from triple annotation with majority voting (Fleiss' κ = 0.66).
    • Benchmarked fine-tuned Banglish-BERT and Gemma-2B (QLoRA) against five zero-shot LLMs; humour detection reached F1 0.708, while sarcasm, offensiveness and vulgarity remained hard because of class imbalance.
    • Applied a MixSarc-tuned classifier to BnSentMix and estimated that about 42% of negative-sentiment samples show sarcastic characteristics.
  • Sep 2024 — Jan 2025

    Zero-shot task-oriented dialogue

    Collaborative research project with Adib Mosharrof, then a PhD student at the University of Kentucky

    • Evaluated GPT-4o as a zero-shot baseline on three unseen Schema-Guided Dialogue domains (hotels, music, restaurants), measuring API-call invocation, API-call correctness and response quality (GLEU).
    • Analysed failure cases: the model invoked API calls reliably but produced fully correct calls less than half the time.
    • Contributed to prompt design and baseline comparison.
